# Privacy Policy — Group Tabs by URL

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

**Group Tabs by URL does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.**

- **What it reads:** the addresses (URLs) of your open tabs, so it can compare them with your rules. This happens on your computer at the moment you group tabs, or when a tab changes address if you turned on automatic grouping. Addresses are never saved or logged.
- **What it stores:** your rules and settings, in Firefox's local extension storage inside your profile. For undo, it also keeps the positions of the tabs it just moved, in temporary session storage that Firefox clears when it closes.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The add-on makes no network requests, contains no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code.
- **Export:** if you click *Export rules*, a file is saved to your computer. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Uninstalling the add-on removes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue on the project page or contact the developer through the add-on's page on addons.mozilla.org.
